## The world

**Name:** Dawn Ledger.

A recovery product photographed like documentary journalism, laid out like a
serious magazine, and instrumented like a lab notebook. The three registers stay
separate and never blur: **photography carries the emotion**, **serif carries the
argument**, **mono carries the measurement**. Nothing decorative sits between them.

The old site was a dark terminal dashboard with no people in it — a product about
oxytocin that showed zero human faces. This world inverts that. Every substantial
page opens on a real person mid-journey. The data language is still there, but it
is now the *smallest* voice on the page rather than the loudest, because that is
the honest hierarchy: the person first, the argument second, the number third.

**Mode by surface:**

| Surface | Mode | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|
| `/`, `/90-r-and-r`, `/aivy`, `/ai4aa`, `/book1`, `/prep90` | Persuade | Visitor downloads the journal or reserves a seat |
| `/the-science`, `/protocol`, `/framework`, `/blog/*`, `/glossary`, `/faq` | Read | Visitor understands the biology and can explain it back |
| `/dashboard`, `/90rr/*`, `/profile`, `/onboarding` | Operate | Visitor logs the day in under 30 seconds |
| `/watch`, `/stories` | Experience | The work leads; interface recedes |

## Colors

Warm near-black, never pure `#000`. Paper white, never pure `#fff`. The old page
was `#050505` on `#f5f5f5` with a red `#dc2626` accent and red radial glows —
cold, clinical, and at odds with a product about warmth and repair.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink-0` | `#0d0f0d` | Page ground |
| `--ink-1` | `#141814` | Raised band / alternating section |
| `--ink-2` | `#1d231d` | Borders, rules, dividers |
| `--ink-3` | `#2a322a` | Hover borders, disabled edges |
| `--paper` | `#f2efe6` | Primary text |
| `--paper-dim` | `#b8b4a6` | Secondary text (tinted from paper, never gray) |
| `--paper-faint` | `#7d7a70` | Tertiary / captions |
| `--green` | `#4cc07a` | Measurement, streaks, "earned", primary CTA |
| `--green-deep` | `#2c7a4d` | Selection, pressed states |
| `--amber` | `#e0a45c` | Dawn accent — italic emphasis, warnings, seats-left |
| `--rust` | `#c2603f` | Crisis and destructive only. Never decorative. |

**Contrast floor:** `--paper-dim` on `--ink-0` is 8.9:1. `--paper-faint` on
`--ink-0` is 4.8:1. `--green` on `--ink-0` is 8.1:1. All pass AA at body size.

**Banned:** red radial background glows, `glow-text`, `glow-border`, colored
zero-offset halos, gradient text, `bg-gradient-to-r` on type.

## Typography

Three faces, three jobs. The old site ran Inter for everything at
`font-black uppercase tracking-widest`, which is the single loudest AI tell in
the codebase and the hardest thing to read for an exhausted newcomer.

| Role | Face | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headings | **Newsreader** (variable, italic) | Weight 500. Italic is the emphasis mechanism, in `--amber`. |
| Body / UI | **Public Sans** | 400/500/600. 17px base, 1.65 line-height. |
| Measurement | **JetBrains Mono** | Only for real numbers, dates, streaks, units, code. Never as a "technical" costume. |

- Body measure: 65–75ch. Never full-bleed paragraphs.
- Display ceiling: 88px desktop / 44px mobile. Tracking floor `-0.025em`.
- **No uppercase body text.** Uppercase is permitted only in the wordmark.
- **No `tracking-widest` on anything.** It was on 40+ elements.

## Photography

The load-bearing element of this world. 16 documentary photographs, one shared
grade, reused across the site (see `src/lib/photos.ts`).

**Grade:** 35mm film grain, muted teal-and-amber, deep near-black shadows, warm
highlight on skin. Every photo must survive being placed on `--ink-0` with no
border.

**Casting rules:**
- Real, imperfect faces. Visible age, skin texture, tiredness.
- Range of ages 20s–50s and ethnicities across the set. No single demographic.
- Mid-action or mid-thought, never posed-to-camera, never stock-smiling.
- Ordinary places: church basements, kitchen tables, porch steps, gravel trails,
  a plain gym, a parked car. No offices, no glass towers, no lifestyle gloss.

**Placement rules:**
- Hero photos carry a bottom-up scrim so text sits on the dark end, never on
  the subject's face.
- Section photos are `5:4` or `4:5` figures with a caption, paired with copy.
- Never animate a photo on hover. Give the container the feedback.
- Every photo needs alt text describing the person and the moment, not the file.

Radii: 10px controls, 14px figures and bands. One elevation declaration per
element — a border **or** a shadow, never both.

## Motion

**One authored moment per page.** Not an entrance fade on every section.

- The craving wave draws itself once when scrolled into view.
- Everything else is state feedback: 180ms color/border transitions, a 0.98
  scale on `:active`.
- All content is **visible at rest**. The old site gated sections behind
  IntersectionObserver opacity, so a fast scroll showed blank black screens.
  Animate in addition to visibility, never instead of it.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the wave draw and smooth scroll.

## Browser surfaces

Themed, not defaulted — this is the cheapest signal a page was built rather than
assembled.

- `::selection` → `--green-deep` on `--paper`
- Scrollbar → `--ink-2` thumb on `--ink-0` track, 3px inset border
- `:focus-visible` → 2px `--green`, 3px offset
- Underline offset 4px on quiet links, `--ink-2` decoration color
- Tabular numerals on all ledger and streak figures

## Copy

- The product's own language: telemetry, the Mirror, the Ledger, BIO 12, DOSE,
  the rooms, the vessel. Keep it — it is earned.
- But **decode it on first use per page.** A newcomer at 1am cannot hold AIV,
  VSE, BIO 12, and D.O.S.E. simultaneously.
- Sentence case in body and buttons. Controls name their action
  ("Download the free journal", not "GET IT NOW").
- Crisis resources (988, 741741, SAMHSA) appear in the footer of every page and
  are never below the fold on `/contact` or `/faq`.

## Refuse

Absolute, no brief earns these back:

- Kickers / eyebrow labels above headings
- Nested cards
- `uppercase tracking-widest` type
- Gradient text
- Pulsing status dots
- Colored `border-left` stripes above 1px
- Red radial background glows
- Emoji standing in for an icon system (use `lucide-react`)
- Content hidden at rest behind scroll observers
